1. **有** (*yǒu*) – Have; there is/are
2. **多少** (*duōshao*) – How many; how much
3. **个** (*gè*) – General measure word
4. **哥哥** (*gēge*) – Older brother
5. **呢** (*ne*) – Used at the end of a question
6. **没有** (*méiyǒu*) – Not have; don't have
7. **家** (*jiā*) – Family; home
8. **几** (*jǐ*) – How many
9. **口** (*kǒu*) – Measure word for family members, knives, etc.
10. **爸爸** (*bàba*) – Father; dad
11. **妈妈** (*māma*) – Mother; mom
12. **妹妹** (*mèimei*) – Younger sister
13. **和** (*hé*) – And
14. **儿子** (*érzi*) – Son
15. **孩子** (*háizi*) – Child; children
16. **女儿** (*nǚ'ér*) – Daughter
17. **岁** (*suì*) – Year of age
18. **他** (*tā*) – He; him
19. **今年** (*jīnnián*) – This year
20. **多** (*duō*) – To what extent; how
21. **大** (*dà*) – (Of age) old; big
