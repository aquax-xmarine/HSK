1. **你好** (_nǐ hǎo_) – Hello 
2. **大家** (_dàjiā_) – Everybody 
3. **好** (_hǎo_) – Good; fine
4. **学生** (_xuésheng_) – Student
5. **们** (_men_) – Plural suffix 
6. **老师** (_lǎoshī_) – Teacher
7. **您** (_nín_) – You (polite)
8. **你们** (_nǐmen_) – You (plural)
9. **谢谢** (_xièxie_) – Thank you
10. **不客气** (_bú kèqi_) – You're welcome
11. **同学** (_tóngxué_) – Classmate
12. **再见** (_zàijiàn_) – Goodbye
