9. **书** (*shū*) – Book
10. **那** (*nà*) – That
11. **第** (*dì*) – Marker of ordinal numbers
12. **学习** (*xuéxí*) – Study; learn
13. **做** (*zuò*) – Do; engage in
14. **白天** (*báitiān*) – Day; daytime
15. **读书** (*dúshū*) – Read a book; study
16. **和** (*hé*) – With
17. **朋友** (*péngyou*) – Friend
18. **唱** (*chàng*) – Sing
19. **歌** (*gē*) – Song
20. **好听** (*hǎotīng*) – Pleasant to hear
21. **电视** (*diànshì*) – Television
22. **狗** (*gǒu*) – Dog
23. **玩** (*wán*) – Play; have fun
